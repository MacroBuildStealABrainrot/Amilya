# Legal and privacy implementation review

Research checked 2026-09-05 against primary Canadian/Ontario and Stripe sources. This records engineering implications and unfinished owner decisions; it is not a legal opinion.

- Meaningful consent requires clear information about what information is collected, the purposes, important third parties and consequences/risks. Sensitive clinical processing needs a context-specific legal assessment; signup acceptance is not patient consent. The privacy draft therefore identifies data categories and optional provider processing, while blocking a blanket compliance claim. [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, consent guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/collecting-personal-information-and-consent/consent/gl_omc_201805/).
- Under Ontario's health-privacy framework the vendor's role and permitted processing depend on its relationship with custodians; electronic service providers acting as agents and health information network providers have different obligations. Establish the actual role and appropriate written agreement rather than assuming the product name determines it. Vendor authority, access/transfer logs, safeguards, subcontractors and breach notification must be addressed in the deployment assessment. [IPC Ontario, virtual health-care visits guidance](https://www.ipc.on.ca/sites/default/files/legacy/2021/02/virtual-health-care-visits.pdf), [IPC Ontario, digital health overview](https://www.ipc.on.ca/en/resources-and-decisions/digital-health-under-phipa-selected-overview).
- Where PIPEDA applies, report breaches posing a real risk of significant harm to the regulator, notify affected individuals, and keep records of all breaches. A written incident process and operator contact are operational launch prerequisites; an API error logger is not that process. [OPC, reporting a business privacy breach](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/report-a-privacy-breach-at-your-organization/report-a-privacy-breach-at-your-business/).
- Ontario internet-agreement disclosure/copy/cancellation requirements must be evaluated for the actual purchasers and transaction. The professional/business context can change consumer-law applicability; do not invent a universal cancellation or refund rule. Owner counsel must confirm the applicable statute in force and payment disclosures before commercial launch. [Ontario Consumer Protection Act, 2002](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/02c30), [Ontario Regulation 17/05](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/050017).
- Stripe snapshot webhooks require raw-body signature verification, durable deduplication and tolerance of delivery retries/order variation. The backend verifies signatures with a five-minute timestamp tolerance, validates mode and explicit API version, serializes workspace balances, and grants paid invoices/checkouts once. Unsupported/missing dependencies fail for retry rather than granting speculative credits. [Stripe webhook guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks), [Stripe subscription events](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks).
- The REST adapter pins stable API version `2026-08-26.dahlia`, listed in the official changelog. Checkout uses server-selected prices, validates the configured CAD monthly amount, and directs existing subscribers to a confirmation flow. [Stripe changelog](https://docs.stripe.com/changelog), [Checkout session creation](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create), [customer portal confirmation flows](https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management/portal-deep-links).

Approve professional/consumer eligibility, tax configuration, refund policy, cancellation/proration configuration and required purchase confirmations. Set a written clinical/support/audit/backup retention schedule and a workable access/export/deletion process. Publish actual hosting regions and subprocessors; execute required agreements and verify provider retention/data-use controls. Confirm local-recovery behavior on managed devices. Establish incident reporting, breach records, access review and customer escalation. Review all privacy/terms text against the final deployed implementation and contracts.
