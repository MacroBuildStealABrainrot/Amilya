# Clinical validation status

This release has **synthetic software verification**, not clinical validation. No real patient records, paid provider calls, diagnostic accuracy study, professional chart review study or prospective clinical evaluation was performed. Live AI execution remains paused by default through `PROVIDER_EXECUTION_ENABLED=false`; no funding or clinical-data approval is implied by these tests. Model IDs in the public provider registry establish public listing only, not account access, zero-retention eligibility or clinical quality.

## What the automated checks establish

`tests/clinical_corpus.json` contains 18 focused extraction scenarios: explicit millimetre conversion; centimetres with absent depth; explicitly measured zero depth; missing units; negative dimensions; unrelated laterality in a nearby sentence; pain severity mistaken for drainage; conditional charting instructions; explicit fever denial; a pain score; explicitly described serous exudate; future fever contingency; diabetes denial; caregiver-reported chills; bilateral location; pain denial; decimal depth without a leading zero; and planned compression with a conditional vascular assessment. These are bounded source-handling examples, not 18 disease diagnoses or a representative clinical population.

`tests/test_clinical.py` also exercises 12 independent input combinations: typed only, manual only, measurement only, photo only, document only, audio only, typed plus photo, photo plus measurement, document plus manual, audio plus document, audio plus photo plus manual, and all six input types. Providers are mocked. The scenarios verify that no required source ordering is imposed and that missing inputs remain absent.

The integration checks cover the following controls:

- Immutable source and fact revision history; explicit clinician confirmation, correction and rejection; rejected and unconfirmed findings excluded from approved documentation.
- Separate wound identities and preservation of repeated medications and record findings. Conflicting confirmed scalar values are surfaced and excluded from approved projection until clinician resolution.
- Finite numeric dimensions with explicit centimetre fields, strict boolean presence, bounded pain scores and tissue percentages, no string-to-number or string-to-boolean coercion, and distinct unknown versus measured zero depth.
- Exact textual source quotations, contextual rejection of conditional, uncertain or historical material as current findings, local negation checks, and verified dimension-unit conversion. General free-text meaning remains subject to clinician review; exact quotation alone does not prove that a model's interpretation is correct.
- Photo suggestions limited to visibly assessable fields; no photo-only dimensions, vascular test or systemic finding accepted. PDF text remains attached to its evidence record; transcript segments and available speaker metadata preserve source attribution. Raw uploaded media are not retained by this implementation.
- Timestamped short-window audio verification with original-file hash matching, local ffprobe/ffmpeg clipping, no original transcript supplied to the speech verifier, separate candidate evidence and explicit clinician acceptance. Accepted excerpts do not replace original transcripts or automatically confirm clinical findings. The configured full Whisper v3 route, explicitly requested medical transcription and labelled repeat fallback are described in [AUDIO_VERIFICATION.md](AUDIO_VERIFICATION.md).
- Durable client-reported photo capture review, explicitly labelled nonclinical. Automatic focus/lighting/highlight metrics require bounded values; manual-review fallback retains absent metrics without fabricating zero measurements. Obstruction review, limitations and image transforms remain evidence metadata, not diagnoses or patient findings.
- Exact UTF-8 note text, whitespace and paragraph preservation; SHA-256 content binding; optimistic case revision checks; atomic signing; idempotent retries; explicit amendments that preserve prior signed text.
- Tenant isolation, stale case rejection, safe clinical audit metadata, and a contextual assistant that retains confirmation states and cannot directly modify facts, notes or signatures.

`tests/test_providers.py` exercises the actual gateway and billing ledger against an isolated SQLite database and mocked HTTP transport: real multimodal content arrays, image metadata removal, zero-retention routing requirements, provider-specific privacy gates, unsupported parameter omission, bounded errors and timeouts, concurrent request replay, encrypted results, reservation before transport, atomic settlement with replay persistence, unusable-output charge waiver, strict usage accounting, three configured speech fallbacks and locally verified audio duration. It also checks that independent documentation QA can reject a proposed draft and that triggered review is reconciled without treating unconfirmed suggestions as approved facts.

`tests/test_audio_verification.py` adds 27 checks for the clip workflow, including a real local decode of synthetic WAV audio in the observed run. `tests/test_media_review.py` adds 14 capture-metadata checks, including the manual fallback. These remain software checks: no real speech provider or clinical patient recording was used. Model pricing and minimum charges are verified separately from clinical performance, and a service's medical-transcription label is not evidence that its output is medically correct.

The 1/10/50/100-user local synthetic handler probe and its measurements are documented in `docs/LOAD_TEST.md`. It uses mocked review, authentication dependency overrides and temporary SQLite databases. It does not validate production capacity or clinical response quality.

## Work required before claims of clinical performance

A separately authorized evaluation should use representative, de-identified or consented cases reviewed by qualified clinicians, explicit reference annotations, predefined safety and usability thresholds, and adjudication of clinically consequential disagreements. It should examine wound types, skin tones, care settings, multisite cases, contradictory and historical records, poor audio and images, uncertain measurements, patient/caregiver attribution, escalation behavior and documentation omissions. Live provider behavior, account access, privacy agreements and retention settings require their own verified approvals. Production operation also requires infrastructure and operational validation beyond local SQLite tests.

Passing these synthetic tests supports the stated software invariants. It does not establish diagnostic sensitivity, specificity, treatment appropriateness, clinical benefit, regulatory clearance or suitability for autonomous care decisions. Clinicians retain responsibility for reviewing the source evidence, resolving uncertainty and signing the final text.
