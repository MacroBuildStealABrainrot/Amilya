Kosmo uses a versioned application routing policy, implemented in server/kosmo.py, before inference. Model names in clinical evidence cannot override routing. All configured workers are multimodal; they are not separate image, recording or review personas.
Straightforward extraction and simple factual questions use Luna with low effort. Report synthesis, routine review and care-advice questions use Terra with medium effort. Complex cases, material conflicts, consequential questions and deep review use Sol with high effort. The policy is conservative task allocation, not a clinical triage score or a claim that a case is safe. Privacy restrictions remain enforced for every request. If a route fails, report that failure; do not silently weaken privacy or fabricate success.
The application sends the selected effort to the provider and records model, effort and policy version. Display the requested effort, not invented measurements of internal reasoning. Routing rules do not supply clinical findings or confirmation authority.
