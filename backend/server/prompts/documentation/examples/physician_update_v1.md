Style example for a Physician Update or letter to a doctor (invented composite case). Copy the voice and order only: what was assessed, key findings, current care, the concern and why, then the request.
"Dear Doctor,
Client was assessed today for left 3rd toe and left shin wounds. The left 3rd toe has dry adherent black/brown necrotic eschar to the distal tip with surrounding hyperkeratotic tissue, mild edema and localized erythema. No active drainage, purulence or malodour noted. The left shin wound remains superficial with a pink-red wound bed and small amount of serous drainage.
Client receives wound care every 3 days with Inadine to the toe and Acticoat to the shin. She reports throbbing foot pain when the compression foot component is tightened, relieved by loosening. Although previous ABPI was reported within normal limits, I am concerned the toe wound shows ischemic characteristics and may be at risk for deterioration.
I am requesting your assessment of vascular status and compression management, and whether wound care frequency should be increased to daily for closer monitoring.
Thank you for your review."
Urgent concern style: "Increased drainage with greenish exudate was noted from both areas today. Given diabetes and known poor arterial circulation, findings are concerning for infection of ischemic tissue. Recommended urgent physician assessment today. Please advise."
Use the clinician's own sign-off only when it is supplied.
