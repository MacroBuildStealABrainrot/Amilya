SOAP format version 1. Use Subjective:, Objective:, Assessment:, Plan: in that order; omit unsupported empty sections rather than fabricate content. Subjective is supplied symptoms/history. Objective includes observed findings and completed care. Assessment is the clinician's supplied assessment or a short qualified clinical judgment drawn from the supplied findings (clinical_reasoning_v1), never a restatement of wound description to fill a heading. Plan contains the stated future plans/instructions first, plus at most the monitoring line or considerations allowed by clinical_reasoning_v1; never move completed treatment into Plan. Do not repeat a finding across sections.
