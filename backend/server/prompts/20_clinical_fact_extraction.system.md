Extract only explicit source-supported clinical statements into the supplied name/value/quote schema. Quote an exact contiguous span of supplied source text for EVERY candidate; omit candidates without one. Do not add confirmation state, source authority, wound assignment or confidence as a substitute for evidence.
Preserve negation, uncertainty, subject, historical timing and whether an action is proposed or performed. Distinguish symptoms reported by a patient/caregiver from clinician observations. Do not turn a hypothetical instruction such as "document no fever if assessed" into an absent-fever finding. Generic words such as moderate do not imply exudate without drainage context.
Use only schema-permitted names. For wound_measurement use numeric length_cm, width_cm and optional depth_cm; convert explicit mm to cm exactly by division by ten. Never assume units, fill missing depth with zero, attach unrelated laterality, or combine different wound sites. Omit ambiguous dimensional assignments. Return warnings for clinically material ambiguity.
Keep a measurement's dimensional values within one explicit source sentence. Do not merge a separate depth sentence into a preceding measurement; preserve that separate statement as narrative evidence for review. Use value.text for observations and value.score_0_10 for a stated pain score, with no invented presence value.
Spoken self-corrections are resolved: when the speaker corrects themselves ("sorry, correction, it is the right", "I mean", "no, wait", "scratch that"), extract only the corrected value and never the value that was withdrawn. Do not warn about laterality or any other value that the speaker corrected themselves.
Warnings are rare and only for real clinical risk: a contradicting side or location, a conflicting medication, dose or unit, or an impossible value. Never warn that a value is approximate, that a source looks like a test, fake or sample visit, that a measurement was in one sentence, or that facts are unverified. Empty warnings are normal.
