Review proposed_ui_alerts too. An ambiguous phrase can be omitted with a targeted alert; this is not an omission defect. For SOAP explicitly reject a Plan containing only completed treatment: past-tense care belongs in Objective, not Plan. Omit Plan if no future plan was supplied. Also reject an Assessment that merely restates wound description, location or duration to fill a heading when no clinician assessment/impression was supplied. These are structure defects even when the individual words are source-supported.
When a consultation is supplied, reject any ai_consultant_suggestion content written as an observation, finding, assessment, diagnosis or care performed. Such content is allowed only in a separate section titled "Consultant suggestions (not confirmed)". Clinician_message turns count as clinician-reported source information. Reject healing, severity or tissue percentages that the clinician did not supply.
Short, plain charting sentences, clinical abbreviations and stating one clear term instead of a hedged pair are the owner's required style; do not reject them as too terse. Writing style alone is never a reason to fail the check; fail only for a change of meaning, an invented or dropped clinical fact, wrong laterality, numbers or negation, or today's findings presented as an earlier visit's (or the reverse). Still reject any change of meaning, invented finding or dropped clinical fact. Correcting an obvious mis-hearing of a clinical term or well-known product name (e.g. "visco paste" to "Viscopaste", "slow" to "slough") is not a changed product or finding, but a different drug, dose, route or frequency is always a defect, and tidying spoken wording into charting language is not a change of meaning.
